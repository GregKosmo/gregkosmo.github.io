Shuttle Instructions

Shuttle pickup and drop-off will be at the Minneapolis aiport at the Terminal 1 Transit Center in the Silver parking ramp. To access the Transit Center:

  • From Terminal 1
    1. From the Baggage Claim, follow signs to “Ground Transportation and Parking”.
    2. Take the escalators down one level.
    3. Take the elevator that is between both escalators to "T".
    4. Exit the elevator, and follow signs for "Buses/Off-site Parking Shuttles".
    5. Continue straight to take the Tram. The Tram (Level T) is one stop only.
    6. Exit the tram and follow signs for silver parking.
    7. Go through the glass doors and take the escalator up, following signs for "Buses/Off-site Parking Shuttles".
    8. Exit the glass doors to the right under the sign saying "Buses".
    9. Look for the "Blue Line Express" shuttle bus.
  • From Terminal 2
    1. From the Baggage Claim, follow signs to Light Rail Transit and go pass check in counter on your left towards the end of the building (Arrivals).
    2. Take the elevator or stairs on your right and follow signs to "Gates H Security Check Point 1 Light Rail Transit Terminal 1 / Orange Parking."

    3. Turn right into the skyway, and at the end of the hallway turn left.

    4. At the end of the walkway turn left go down the stairs into the Light Rail Transit Ramp. No fare needed between Terminals.
    5. Turn left and make sure to look for signs for Terminal Lindbergh / Downtown Minneapolis.
    6. Continue straight to take the Tram. The Tram (Level T) is one stop only.
    7. Exit the tram and follow signs for silver parking.
    8. Go through the glass doors and take the escalator up, following signs for "Buses/Off-site Parking Shuttles".
    9. Exit the glass doors to the right under the sign saying "Buses".
    10. Look for the "Blue Line Express" shuttle bus.
