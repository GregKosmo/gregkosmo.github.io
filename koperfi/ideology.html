Our mission

The tools created here aim to give you greater knowledge and control over your personal finances and future. They should empower you to find that understanding your financial situation is achievable, and that armed with that knowledge, you can achieve whatever goals you want. I believe lacking financial security really means lacking time and resources, and that these tools will give you all three.

Who this is for

I'm not really sure. When I started working my first real job, I had more debt than savings. I studied all the personal finance info I could, and developed savings plans which worked for me. As I grew older, I changed jobs, started investing, got married, and my wife and I bought a house and started a business. With the complexity of life came complexity of our finances. Your life is probably complex too. If you find yourself struggling to understand whether you are saving enough to reach your goals, whether you can truly afford to make a purchase right now, or what changes you'd need to make in your life to become financially secure, you aren't any different from anyone else. Regardless of who these tools help, that is who they were built by and for.

How it works

You should find some consistent principles through the services offered here. Mainly speed, simplicity, and privacy. While products are built with those in mind, they won't sacrifice quality or accuracy in their results. Most products will likely involve some setup questions to get a better understanding about your situation, then will provide an interface where you can easily make changes and get information. Every service is offered for free, paid for with ads. Paid services will be offered for cloud data storage and sharing data between accounts. Unless you pay for those cloud services, information will be stored entirely on your device.
